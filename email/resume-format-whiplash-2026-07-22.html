Your resume didn't get worse. The rules did.

The job search forums are relitigating resume formatting this week, with a sharp new edge: people whose resumes used to get callbacks are hearing silence, without changing a thing. Three things shifted.

Length now depends on seniority

One page for zero to five years, two for five to fifteen, two to three at executive level. The old one page rule is not universal anymore.

Trade STAR for Action plus Result

On the resume, lead with what you did and what happened. Save the full Situation and Task setup for interviews, where you have room for it.

Old resumes are quietly failing

ATS parsing changed this year. If yours has not been touched in twelve months, re-check it and drop dense tables, columns and graphics from the version you submit through portals.
