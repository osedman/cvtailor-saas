## Theme 1 — The AI Hiring Black Box

**What the community is saying:** ~6 in 10 job seekers say the most frustrating part of applying is not knowing whether a human ever saw their resume; about half are rejected with zero explanation, most assuming an algorithm made the call. Candidates aren't anti-AI on principle, but resent zero-visibility decisions after real time invested tailoring. A darker thread is emerging about AI possibly favoring AI-polished resumes over human-written ones.

**Best practices:**
- Apply through a named contact or referral where possible, it routes around blind automated screening
- Write for a human first, then sanity-check ATS formatting
- Check any status portal before assuming silence means rejection
- Ask directly in a follow-up whether a human reviews applications at that stage
- Keep a shortlist of companies known for transparent hiring
- Treat silence as a data point about the employer, not a verdict on your worth

## Theme 2 — Recruiter Ghosting & Broken Communication

**What the community is saying:** Three-week silences after screening calls, aggressive decision deadlines followed by recruiters vanishing, and handoffs where the new contact never re-engages. Attributed partly to internal recruiter-team churn, experienced recruiters leaving or being laid off while remaining staff are stretched thin.

**Best practices:**
- Get a named contact's direct email early
- Set your own follow-up cadence, day 7 and day 14, rather than waiting indefinitely
- If given a tight deadline, ask in writing what happens if you need more time
- After a handoff, proactively email the new recruiter within 48 hours
- Keep a simple log of who you spoke to, when, and what was promised
- Treat consistently poor pre-offer communication as a preview of post-hire management

## Theme 3 — Resume Format Whiplash

**What the community is saying:** The one-page rule is being replaced by a length-by-seniority consensus, one page for 0 to 5 years, two pages for 5 to 15, two to three for executive. STAR is being trimmed to Action + Result for resume bullets specifically, full STAR still recommended for interviews. A harder claim is gaining traction that resumes which used to pass are now failing, because ATS filters and recruiter search logic have shifted in the last year.

**Best practices:**
- Match resume length to career stage, not habit
- Lead resume bullets with Action + Result rather than full Situation-Task-Action-Result
- Re-check anything unchanged for 12+ months against a current ATS-compatibility checker
- Avoid dense tables, columns, and graphics in portal submissions
- Save full STAR for interview answers
- Tailor per role rather than defaulting to one master template
