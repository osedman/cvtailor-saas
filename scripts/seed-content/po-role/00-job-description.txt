Product Owner — Digital Patient Services
Meridian Health · London (hybrid, 2 days a week on site in Fitzrovia) · £70,000–£80,000 + bonus
Recruiting agency: Halcyon Search

ABOUT THE ROLE
Meridian Health is a UK private healthcare group running outpatient clinics, diagnostics centres and two surgical hospitals. Around 60,000 patients a month now book, pay, check in and read their results through our digital channels: the Meridian app, the patient portal and online booking on our website.

We are looking for a Product Owner to lead the Digital Patient Services team: seven engineers, a designer, a QA and a part-time user researcher. You will own what the team builds and why, working day to day with clinicians, clinic operations, contact centre leads and our clinical safety officer. This is a hands-on role: you will write the stories, run the refinement, and be accountable for the numbers.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Own and prioritise the backlog for booking, the patient portal and the app
- Set the quarterly roadmap and the outcome metrics it is measured against
- Turn clinical and operational needs into clear, testable user stories
- Use product analytics to decide what to build next and to show what worked
- Work with our platform suppliers on integrations, releases and SLAs

WHAT YOU'LL NEED

Must have
1. At least three years owning a product backlog as a Product Owner in a Scrum team
2. Strong writing of user stories and acceptance criteria that engineers can build and test against
3. Stakeholder management with clinicians and operational teams, including handling competing priorities
4. Experience delivering patient-facing or customer-facing digital products such as online booking, portals or mobile apps
5. Data-informed prioritisation using product analytics (Amplitude, GA4 or similar) and enough SQL to answer your own questions
6. Ownership of a product roadmap tied to measurable outcome metrics

Nice to have
7. Knowledge of healthcare regulation and assurance: DSPT, clinical safety standards DCB0129/DCB0160, and UK GDPR for health data
8. Working knowledge of accessibility standards, ideally WCAG 2.2 AA
9. Experience working closely with UX research, including planning and acting on usability studies
10. Vendor or supplier management, including contracts, roadmaps and service levels

WHAT WE OFFER
- £70,000–£80,000 base, plus up to 10% annual bonus
- Hybrid working: two days a week in our Fitzrovia office, the rest remote
- Private medical cover through Meridian, 27 days' holiday plus bank holidays
- £1,500 annual learning budget

HOW TO APPLY
Halcyon Search is recruiting this role on behalf of Meridian Health. Interviews are in two stages: a conversation with the Head of Digital, then a prioritisation exercise with the team.
