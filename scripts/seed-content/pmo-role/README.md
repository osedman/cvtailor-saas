# Seed content: PMO Analyst (undergraduate placement), twenty candidates

These are paste-ready CVs for **ROL-2422, PMO Analyst**, a placement-year
role on staging (Halcyon Search; Frimley, at least 3 days a week on site).
The role was created by Yemi.

They are written to the role's ten requirements. R01 to R05 are weighted
important:

- R01: right to work in the UK
- R02: currently studying for an undergraduate degree
- R03: Word, PowerPoint and Excel
- R04: communication
- R05: organisation

R06 to R10 are nice-to-haves: business improvement, PRINCE2/Agile/PMI,
Smartsheet, Microsoft Project, and dashboards.

at school.
