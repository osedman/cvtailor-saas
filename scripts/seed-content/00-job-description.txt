Senior Data Engineer — Patient Platform

We are rebuilding the data layer that sits under it. You would own the pipelines
that move clinical and operational data between services, and the models the
analytics team builds on.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Design and own batch and streaming pipelines moving ~2TB/day
- Rebuild our dbt models and take responsibility for data quality and testing
- Work directly with clinical safety and information governance colleagues
- Mentor two junior engineers

WHAT WE NEED
- Strong commercial Python, five years or more
- Production experience with Apache Airflow or an equivalent orchestrator
- Deep SQL, including query tuning on large tables
- dbt in production, not just evaluated
- Cloud data warehouse experience: Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift
- Streaming experience: Kafka, Kinesis or similar
- Infrastructure as code, ideally Terraform
- Experience handling personal or special category data under UK GDPR
- Prior work in healthcare, clinical or another regulated domain
- Evidence of mentoring or leading engineers

NICE TO HAVE
